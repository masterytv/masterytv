C · “The Tether” — two of us, holding one line

Born from the double-t in relatti: two upright stems — two people standing on their own — joined by one continuous line that gently dips between them. The dip is honest: the line between two people carries weight. It’s also the retention thesis drawn as a mark (the external stake, held at both ends). The most ownable and most “premium editorial” of the three; the least instantly romantic.

Recommendation: A for launch. For a 20-person beta recruited off Reddit, the mark’s first job is category recognition at scroll speed — A keeps the heart’s instant read while being ownable (“two halves, and the overlap is us”). It’s also the best storyteller in the invite email and share card. B is the purest concept and the best platform mark if the same identity must someday cover career coaching. C is the most distinctive and most premium-editorial, and pairs beautifully with the “tt” in the wordmark — a strong candidate to grow into after launch.

All three follow the brand spec: single hue family (the Relatti rose ramp), flat color, no gradient blobs, no clipart, judged at 16px first.

## 1. The reframe (the hero)

> **The hero is the user's *edge* — the version of them that makes clean, powerful money decisions. Money is the arena; their psychology is the lever; the assessment is the instrument. What's demoted: budgets, bank data, net worth, and "financial advice." We never touch the bank account. We work on what's upstream of every number: the beliefs, fears, and patterns that decide what they do with money before they know they've decided.**

One sentence for the market: **"The coach that works on what's *under* your earning, spending, and pricing — because your bank account is a symptom, not the problem."**

This is the axis split that makes us not-Cleo, not-a-budgeting-app, and not-a-manifestation-guru: **psychology, not your bank account; science, not woo; an edge, not a healing.**

## 2. Who we are moving, and what actually moves them

The founder's own live UX test (KMSI-R → 3 LLMs → his story → advice; captured in [MONEY_EXPERIENCE_NOTES.md](MONEY_EXPERIENCE_NOTES.md) §1) *demonstrated* the mechanism:

- A test alone produces a **commodity** — competent, generic, forgettable.
- The instant a **story** is added, the same scores get reread and the experience turns magical ("this changes things").
- What hooks is **the reveal** — the coach naming something true the user hadn't said out loud.
- What converts a reader into a member is **applying it to a real decision.**

So the entire experience is engineered to reach a reveal fast, then keep manufacturing them. **The test is the Trojan horse. The story is the product. The reveal is the moment.**

## 3. The evidence base (and our authority strategy)

**The science we build on.** The most validated money-psychology framework is the money-belief pattern research (four recurring patterns: avoidance, worship, status, vigilance), which predicts real outcomes — net worth, debt, financial behavior. Federal data (CFPB National Financial Well-Being study) independently supports the founder's core thesis: **psychological and behavioral factors — confidence, habits, day-to-day money behaviors — associate with financial well-being more strongly than financial *knowledge* does.** You cannot fix this with a course. That is the whole reason a coach beats a class.

**Our authority strategy (founder decision, 2026-07-17): results, not credentials.** We do **not** license an academic instrument or lean on citations for authority. Consumer trust in this niche comes from the reveal landing ("it read me in three minutes") and from **outcome testimonials**, which are more persuasive to our audience than any journal reference. We build our own instrument (§4) and earn authority by showing it works.

> **The one honest engineering floor under that strategy:** results-based authority *requires the results to be real*, which requires the instrument to actually measure something. A vibes-only quiz produces reveals that miss — the exact opposite of the goal, and trust dies on the first bad read. So we skip academic publication and citation-authority (agreed), but we keep enough **instrument hygiene** (face validity, internal consistency, test-retest sanity on the first cohort) that the types are real and the quarterly deltas mean something. **The elegant part:** our engine already re-administers assessments and tracks deltas over time — so *operating the product generates both the marketing proof and the instrument's own validation evidence at once.* Our longitudinal data becomes the moat and the credential.

## 4. Money Maps™ — our instrument (the new build the founder called for)

**Decision: build our own, based on the money-belief research backbone, with our own construct names, our own item verbiage, and viable entrepreneur-tuned differences.** The four underlying *constructs* are ideas (not copyrightable); the *items* and the *name* "Money Scripts®" are Klontz's and we use neither. "Money Maps™" is ours to trademark.

### 4.1 Why it's genuinely different, not a paraphrase
The classic four patterns were validated on the *general population* and framed around **dysfunction** (avoidance and worship as problems). Money Maps is re-architected for **entrepreneurs seeking an edge**:
- **Strengths-with-a-governor framing.** Every Map is an *asset with an overclock*, not a pathology. ("Your caution is a superpower, slightly overclocked" is the register — never "here's what's wrong with you.")
- **Entrepreneur-native items.** Situations are pricing a proposal, a launch, losing a big client, a windfall, raising vs. bootstrapping — not general-population money scenarios.
- **A signature dimension the classic four under-weight** (§4.3) — the piece that made the founder's own reveal land.

### 4.2 The four core Maps (working names — final copy is a §11 deliverable)
| Map | Rooted in | The edge (superpower) | The overclock (cost) |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **GUARD** | vigilance | discipline, resilience, never blows up | worry that won't update, can't enjoy, fear-based "no"s |
| **DRIVE** | worship/focus | ambition, work ethic, momentum | the moving goalpost, overwork, "I'll relax when…" |
| **MIRROR** | status | standards, drive to level up | image-spending, comparison, self-worth riding on the number |
| **SHADOW** | avoidance | values-grounded, immune to greed | undercharging, avoiding the numbers, "I'm fine with less" as a hidden cage |

### 4.3 The signature dimension (our "viable difference")
- **THE LEAP** — how you relate to *downside* **and** to *your own success.* Fear of failure and fear of success are distinct forces and both cap founders; the classic four scripts barely touch them. This is the dimension behind the transcript's killer reveal ("afraid of becoming the version of yourself success required"). **This is Money Maps' signature — the thing a generic money-beliefs test can't tell you.**

> **✅ DIMENSIONS LOCKED (founder, 2026-07-17): 5 dimensions = the 4 core Maps + THE LEAP. No 6th (ANCHOR/identity-fusion folds into MIRROR + LEAP).** Full instrument (items, scoring, archetypes, card copy) is now built in ⭐ **[MONEY_MAPS_INSTRUMENT.md](MONEY_MAPS_INSTRUMENT.md)** — the implementation spec.

### 4.4 Output: the typology (the shareable engine)
Dominant Map × secondary Map → a **named archetype** with a card ("The Relentless Builder" = DRIVE-GUARD; "The Reluctant Rainmaker" = SHADOW-DRIVE; etc.). ~12–20 named types — enough to feel personal, few enough to build card art for. The card carries **one edge and one leak** (never flattery-only; accurate discomfort is what makes it shareable and credible — see `MONEY_VIRAL_GTM.md` §5). Archetype naming is a §11 copy deliverable; the derivation logic (dominant×secondary) is fixed here.

### 4.5 Item + scoring rules
- **12–16 items** (the friction budget from §6), 1–6 agreement or forced-choice; our verbiage only.
- Each item **self-revealing** — the user learns about themselves *as they answer* (a reason the quiz reads as the ride, not the toll).
- Scored in **code**, deterministic; produces the four+ Map scores → dominant/secondary → archetype. (Reuses the Decoded scoring-engine pattern; `DECODED_SCORING.md` discipline: item text is canonical once shipped — changing it invalidates stored responses.)
- **Progress metric (no bank data):** re-administer Money Maps quarterly and show the overclock dials coming down, plus a short money-anxiety/decision-confidence check. (Better-fit for this segment than the CFPB well-being scale, which is built around making-ends-meet; keep CFPB FWBS as an optional secondary.)

## 5. The personalization model (why generic competitors can't copy us)

The coach's intelligence at any moment = **Money Map type × the user's story × their present situation.** We *know who this person is* — their dominant Maps, their overclocks, their archetype, and (as it accrues) their story and pattern history. A generic money chatbot knows none of it. Every coach turn is tuned by:
- **The type** → which single question fits, which reframe lands, what to watch for (a DRIVE-GUARD gets "does 'enough' have a number?"; a SHADOW gets "what's the money task you keep not doing?").
- **The story** → the origin the coach reflects back (the crash, the awakening, the parent who modeled scarcity).
- **The situation** → the live decision the whole profile gets applied to (§8).
- **The history** → longitudinal patterns ("this feeling has preceded your best moves before").

## 6. The journey — the Reveal Ladder (the spine)

**Entry decision, LOCKED (founder, 2026-07-17): quiz first → results in chat → the coach's questions carry pre-written clickable answer chips + an always-present free-text box.** ("Chips to move fast, free-text to go deep — the user picks the depth every turn.") Rationale and the full argument: `MONEY_EXPERIENCE_NOTES.md` §3. Design target: **minimize time-to-first-reveal, not step count.**

| Rung | User gives | Coach returns (the payoff that buys the next rung) | Gate |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **Hook** | attention (10s) | one sharp, specific line in the coach's voice — *not* a landing-page pitch — then "give me 3 minutes and I'll tell you something true about you" | none |
| **0 — Money Map** | 12–16 taps | named archetype + shareable card, framed as a **hypothesis, not a verdict**: "here's the read — but a score lies without a story. Want me to check if it's true for you?" | none |
| **1 — The one question** | one tap/line to a type-selected question | the engineered **"this changes things"** — a reveal fusing score × answer | soft email/magic-link: *"want me to remember this?"* |
| **2 — Money Story** | the narrative, via chips + free-text; coach goes first on the shame | pattern reflection — the coach names the throughline | — |
| **3 — Situation + the fork** | where they are + the live decision | the coach applies the whole profile to a **real decision** (§8) | full account / consent for memory |
| **4 — Money OS** | goals, values, "enough," guardrails — co-created | the living document begins (§9); the coaching relationship is now real | paid tier |

Each rung is a valid stopping point that already delivered value, so the next rung is an invitation, not a wall. A secondary **chat-first door** exists for the quiz-averse, with the coach steering to the Map fast.

## 8. The Decision Room (the hero use case — recommended V1 spine)

> ⚠️ **Founder confirm:** I'm speccing the **Decision Room** as the V1 spine over a daily-question habit loop, per the recommendation in the notes. Overwrite if you want the daily loop to lead.

Everyone builds these products around a morning-question loop. But what hooked the founder was a **real decision** ("build again or get a job?"). Entrepreneurs face money-psychology decisions constantly — raise or bootstrap, take the deal, drop the price, hire or wait, quit. The Decision Room is a "think this through with me" mode where the user brings a live decision and the coach applies their whole Money Map profile to it. It is **higher-value, more defensible, more viral** ("I make my big calls with this thing"), and the truest expression of the edge. The daily/proactive layer still exists — but as the *supporting* rhythm, not the headline.

Decision Room mechanics: user names the decision → coach surfaces the relevant Maps/overclocks in play ("your DRIVE is loud here; is this the goalpost moving, or a real opportunity?") → pressure-tests against their stated values and guardrails → **is willing to disagree** (the anti-sycophancy rule; catching "just this once" exceptions) → leaves a written decision record in the Money OS.

## 9. The Money OS (the living document — retention spine)

A single evolving manual the coach maintains and the user watches grow: *who I am at my best · my self-sabotage patterns · my triggers · what restores me · my non-negotiables · my "enough" number · my mission · my decision log.* Both LLMs in the founder's test converged on this. It is the retention spine (leaving means abandoning a version of yourself), the exportable proof (hand it to an advisor/partner), and the most screenshot-worthy object in the product. First-class data object; updated every meaningful interaction.

## 10. Coach voice + first message (`/edge` register)

- **Register:** sharp, specific, respects their intelligence, unafraid to disagree. Reframes traits as **edges with governors**, not wounds. The **Fable 5 response in the founder's transcript is the golden fixture** — held the score as a hypothesis, reread it through the story, named the unspoken fear, pushed back honestly, offered a concrete artifact. The coach-lab battery targets that quality bar.
- **First message** is the reveal off the Money Map, not "Hi, I'm your coach." It says something true and slightly uncomfortable, then asks the ONE type-selected question (with chips).
- **Non-negotiables:** hypothesis-not-verdict; one question at a time; willing to disagree; earns each disclosure; **never gives securities/financial-planning advice** (`MONEY_DISCOVERY.md` §6.1 — coach the *psychology* of the decision, refer out for the finance); crisis kernel on (money-shame correlates with crisis); coaching-not-therapy and not-financial-advice framing explicit.
- Built as a **Coach Pack** (`_shared/packs/money-pack.ts`) over the shared kernel — editing it cannot touch the executive or relationship coaches (PC4 seam). Add `money` to both `ProgramId` unions, follow the compile errors (tenancy T0–T7).

## 13. Safety & boundaries

- Crisis kernel on (shared, never forked — PC4.5); money-shame/financial-ruin patterns added to the shared kernel; financial ruin correlates with crisis states.
- **Anti-sycophancy is a spec'd, tested behavior** — the coach must push back, especially on a desperate all-in money bet. Sycophancy is the failure mode for this exact psychology.
- **Coaching, not therapy. Not financial/investment advice.** The coach opines on the *psychology* of a money decision; it refers out for clinical and financial-planning needs; disclaimers per `MONEY_DISCOVERY.md` §6. No bank linking in V1 (differentiator *and* the lighter data posture).
